Go Knowledge Press Transformation

Project Overview

Go Knowledge Press (GKP) is a Japanese publishing company specialising in books about the strategy board game Go. The company planned to expand into Western markets, including Europe, the United States, Canada, Australia, and New Zealand.

Their existing website relied heavily on static pages, email orders, fax orders, and konbini payments, creating significant barriers for international growth.

The project focused on designing a scalable digital transformation strategy that improved customer experience, operational efficiency, and international accessibility.

Business Challenges

Outdated Ordering System

Orders were processed manually through email, fax, and traditional Japanese payment methods, limiting scalability and usability.

Poor Mobile Experience

The existing static website lacked responsive design, creating friction for modern mobile users.

No CRM or ERP Integration

The business lacked operational systems for customer management, analytics, and inventory coordination.

International Expansion Constraints

Existing infrastructure could not support increased global traffic, transactions, or multilingual customer expectations.

Research & Analysis

The project combined SWOT analysis, competitor benchmarking, and digital infrastructure evaluation to identify key operational weaknesses and transformation opportunities.

User Personas

User personas were developed to understand the expectations and purchasing behaviour of Western customers unfamiliar with traditional Japanese ordering systems.

Competitive Go Player

Experienced strategy game enthusiast looking for specialist translated books, fast checkout, and international shipping.

Casual Learner

Newer customers requiring clear navigation, beginner-friendly categorisation, and simple purchasing workflows.

International Collector

Interested in premium and rare publications, requiring secure payments, trust signals, and reliable global delivery.

UX & eCommerce Strategy

The proposed platform prioritised conversion optimisation, responsive design, and simplified international purchasing workflows.

Responsive Design

Mobile-first layouts ensured accessibility across smartphones, tablets, and desktop devices.

Advanced Search & Filtering

Product categorisation and filtering systems improved discoverability for niche publications.

Simplified Checkout

Modern payment methods reduced friction compared to traditional manual ordering systems.

Trust & Internationalisation

The interface incorporated multilingual support, transparent shipping information, and familiar Western eCommerce patterns.

Platform Evaluation

Multiple eCommerce platforms were evaluated based on scalability, maintenance complexity, operational cost, and non-technical usability.

Why Shopify?

Shopify was selected due to its scalability, integrated payment systems, strong plugin ecosystem, and ease of use for non-technical staff.

Operational Simplicity

SaaS infrastructure reduced maintenance overhead and removed the need for dedicated server management.

Marketing Integration

Shopify integrations supported SEO, customer analytics, email marketing, and promotional campaigns.

Scalability

The platform could support increased traffic, inventory growth, and international transactions over time.

Payment & Shipping Strategy

A major focus of the transformation strategy involved reducing purchasing friction for Western customers.

Flexible Payments

Recommended payment methods included credit/debit cards, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and Buy Now Pay Later solutions.

Global Shipping

International shipping integrations improved transparency, delivery tracking, and operational efficiency.

Free Shipping Incentives

Free shipping thresholds were proposed to increase average order value and improve conversion rates.

Currency & Localisation

Multi-currency support improved usability for international customers.

User journeys and storefront structures were mapped to improve product discovery, purchasing flow, and customer trust.

Digital Infrastructure Strategy

The proposed infrastructure strategy focused on long-term scalability, operational visibility, and customer insight generation.

CRM Integration

Customer relationship management tools enabled audience segmentation, personalised communication, and retention strategies.

ERP Integration

Inventory and fulfilment systems improved operational coordination and stock visibility.

Analytics & Reporting

Customer analytics supported data-driven marketing and product strategy decisions.

Cloud Hosting

Cloud-based infrastructure improved reliability, scalability, and global accessibility.

Risk Assessment

The project critically evaluated operational and technical risks associated with digital transformation and international expansion.

Operational Complexity

Expanding internationally increased fulfilment and customer support demands.

Platform Dependency

SaaS platforms introduced long-term dependency on subscription pricing and platform limitations.

Logistics Challenges

International shipping costs and delivery times could impact customer satisfaction.

Maintenance & Growth

Ongoing optimisation and content management would require operational investment over time.

Future Opportunities

AI Product Recommendations

Personalised recommendation systems could improve engagement and upselling.

Community Features

Discussion forums and user reviews could strengthen customer loyalty.

Subscription Services

Membership models could provide recurring revenue opportunities.

Expanded Internationalisation

Additional language support and regional storefronts could support global growth.
